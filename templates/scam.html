Overcharging Scams

Tourists are often targeted by unscrupulous vendors and service providers who overcharge for goods and services. Here are some common overcharging scams and ways to avoid them:

Taxi Scams

Some taxi drivers may take a longer route to your destination in order to run up the meter, or they may use a meter that has been tampered with to charge more than the fair rate. To avoid this scam, it's a good idea to use a reputable taxi company or ride-sharing service, and to agree on the fare before getting in the taxi.

Fake Tour Scams

Some tour operators may offer a low price for a tour, but then pressure tourists into paying extra for various "optional" activities or services. To avoid this scam, it's a good idea to research tour operators and read reviews before booking a tour.
